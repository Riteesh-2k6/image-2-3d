# GeoPrior

The 3D reconstruction and provenance pipeline for converting drone video and geometric priors into verified 3D meshes.

## Provenance

**Observed**:
A primitive verified by ≥2 independent camera views from the drone footage.
_Avoid_: Verified, Exported

**Prior-Guided**:
A primitive derived from map providers (Overture Maps, OpenStreetMap, Cesium Terrain) without sufficient camera verification.
_Avoid_: Unverified, Map data

**AI-Inferred**:
A primitive generated by AI models without explicit geometric priors or sufficient observation.
_Avoid_: Hallucinated

## Pipeline

**SSAKS (Semantic Scene-Aware Keyframe Selection)**:
The initial video processing stage that filters redundant frames using optical flow, blur detection, and DINO novelty clustering.

**Speedy-Splat**:
The core 3D Gaussian Splatting engine, strictly optimized to run within a 6 GB VRAM budget.

**SERE (Surface Extraction and Regularization Engine)**:
The final pipeline stage that converts the Gaussian point cloud into a clean, topology-repaired mesh using SuGaR and constraint optimization.

**Live Mode**:
The real-time, ephemeral rendering mode used during drone flight to visualize reconstruction progress and confidence.

**Offline Mode**:
The canonical reconstruction pipeline that processes raw video and keyframes post-flight.
